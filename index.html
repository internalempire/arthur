Heart–Lung Interaction

Real-time cardiopulmonary simulator

A closed-loop lumped-parameter model. Every compartment is referenced to the pressure that surrounds it, so the interactions shown are emergent rather than scripted. Built from Kenny, An Approach to Mechanical Heart-Lung Interaction (2020); Mahmood & Pinsky, Ann Transl Med 2018;6:349; Yuriditsky, Mireles-Cabodevila & Alviar, ATS Scholar 2025;6:94–108.

For teaching only. This is a simplification of human physiology and must not be used to guide the care of a patient.
